# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Caveman Linux is a learning community. Be respectful, patient, constructive, and welcoming to people of every experience level and background.

Positive behaviour includes explaining corrections with evidence, giving actionable feedback, acknowledging different learning needs, and focusing criticism on content rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and insulting or demeaning conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope and Enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces representing the project. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject participation that violates it.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers should protect reporters' privacy and respond proportionately and fairly.
